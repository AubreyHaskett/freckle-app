What Freckle does

Grown-up work for your skin.

Photo-based tracking

Photograph moles, spots, and skin changes at consistent intervals. Compare images side by side across weeks and months to see what, if anything, has shifted.

Measurements and history

Log width, height, and body location for each spot. Freckle tracks when you last checked in and reminds you when it's time to take another look.

Stays on your device

No account required. No internet connection needed. Your photos, notes, and measurement history live in your phone's private storage and nowhere else.

Privacy by design

Your photos never leave your phone.

Most apps in this category want your data. Freckle doesn't. There's no server to send it to. No account to store it in. No analytics watching what you do.

This is a deliberate choice — skin photography is personal. It should stay that way.
