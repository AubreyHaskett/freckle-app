Privacy by design

We never see your data.

Most apps in this category want your photos. Freckle doesn't. There's no Freckle server, no account, and no analytics watching what you do. Your data is yours — stored on your device and optionally backed up to your own Google Drive.

This is a deliberate choice — skin photography is personal. You should be the only one who has it.
